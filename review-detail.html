Titanium Pro Max review: almost flawless.

By David Chen
Updated Sept 15, 2026 9 min read
Editorial score
9.2
Voltwise lab · out of 10
Reader rating 4.5/5 · 1,284 reviews

Pros & cons breakdown

Display

Pros

  • 2,500-nit panel stays readable in direct sun
  • Smooth, adaptive 120Hz refresh

Cons

  • Rivals now offer 144Hz at this price

Performance

Pros

  • Unbeatable sustained performance
  • Stays cool during long gaming sessions

Cons

  • 256GB base storage fills fast with 4K video

Battery

Pros

  • Two-day battery life for most users
  • Very low overnight standby drain

Cons

  • Slow 27W wired charging compared to rivals

Camera

Pros

  • Exceptional low-light capability
  • Sharp, usable 5x telephoto

Cons

  • Software sometimes over-sharpens fine detail

Build & value

Pros

  • Premium titanium build feels lighter than expected
  • Five years of promised software updates

Cons

  • Very expensive starting price
  • Heavier than compact rivals at 221g

The verdict

The Titanium Pro Max refines an already excellent formula with better battery life and a stunning display. It's an iterative update, but it's executed perfectly.

Who it's forPower users and mobile photographers who demand the best battery life.
Who should skipAnyone who bought last year's model, or people looking for a lightweight device.
