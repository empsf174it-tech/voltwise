How it works

From unboxing to verdict
in four steps.

Every review published on Voltwise follows the same process, so scores are comparable across brands and years.

01

Source

We buy retail units wherever possible, or accept loans only under strict no-review-approval terms.

02

Test

Standardized lab tests cover display, battery, thermals and cameras, so results are comparable across brands.

03

Live with it

A reviewer uses it as their main device for at least a week to find what lab tests miss.

04

Verdict & publish

Findings become a pros & cons breakdown and a verdict, peer-reviewed by a second editor. Reader reviews follow.
