About Voltwise

Cutting through the noise.
Clarity in a crowded market.

We're a small, obsessive team of engineers and writers who believe buying tech should be informed by data, not hype.

Our mission

To deliver unbiased, comprehensive, and accessible electronics reviews that empower people to make smart purchasing decisions.

Our promise

Total editorial independence. We never accept paid reviews. Scores come solely from lab data and real-world use.

Our transparency

Every score links back to its methodology. If a brand loans us a device, we say so — right at the top.

How we test

A gauntlet of tests before it ever sees a pocket.

Numbers only tell half the story. After the lab, devices go home with a reviewer as their primary device for at least 7 days, revealing the bugs, ergonomic flaws, and quality-of-life features benchmarks miss.

  • Sustained performance — 30-minute stress loops to expose throttling.
  • Display accuracy — Delta-E colour measurement and peak brightness.
  • Battery curves — standardized rundown and charge-rate tests.
  • Daily-driver week — real life, real pockets, real bags.
